Sitara

A freshly new way of creating music for her creeps in, taking the form of PVC. OUT NOW: Noche Serena reshapes vinyl records into a piece made for lulling into a serene night.

Sitara, a Malaysian-American artist from New York, combines strings, electronics, and vocals to create lush soundscapes. Listening is like drifting through a half-remembered dream. This comes alive visually through pointillism in her notebook.
